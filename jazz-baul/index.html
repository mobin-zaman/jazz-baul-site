About

Jazz Baul Records is an independent sound studio and audio production house in Dhaka, founded by Akram Siddiquee.

Built on more than a decade of working with sound, the studio covers the full arc of audio — music production, sound design, recording, mixing and mastering — across music, film, animation, games and commercial projects.

The room is built for artists and projects that want their sound taken seriously: clean recording, considered production, and mixes that translate everywhere.
