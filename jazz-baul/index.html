~ The Studio ~

About

Jazz Baul Records is the independent studio and audio production space founded by Akram Siddiquee in Dhaka. It works across music, film, animation, games and commercial projects.

Built on more than a decade of working with sound, the studio covers the full arc of making and shaping audio — from writing and producing songs to sound design, recording, mixing and mastering.

It is also the label home for AKRAM's own records.
