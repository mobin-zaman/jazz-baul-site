~ The studio ~

About

Jazz Baul Records is an independent sound studio and audio production house in Dhaka, founded by Akram Siddiquee. It works across music, film, animation, games and commercial projects.

Built on more than a decade of working with sound, the studio covers the full arc of audio — from writing and producing music to sound design, recording, mixing and mastering.

The room is built for artists and projects that want their sound taken seriously: clean recording, considered production, and mixes that translate everywhere.
