~ The Story ~

Akram Siddiquee

I go where the বাউল road meets the জ্যাজ night.

Akram Siddiquee — on stage and on record, Jazz Baul — is a singer-songwriter and producer from Dhaka. He works the space between Baul folk and jazz, singing in Bengali and English, often both in the same verse.

By day he's a working sound designer with over a decade in the studio: film foley, spatial audio, mixing sessions for other artists in the city. By night the guitar comes out and the songs take over. He runs Jazz Baul Records, plays around Song Zone and BackStage Club, and studied at North South University. The journey's been going since 2018.
