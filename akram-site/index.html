About AKRAM

Akram Siddiquee is a Bangladeshi singer-songwriter, music producer and sound designer working across music, film, animation and games.

His work moves between songwriting, production and sound — from intimate vocal performances and guitar-driven songs to cinematic sound design and audio production.

With more than a decade of experience working with sound, Akram has worked across independent music, film, animation, advertising and game audio. He is also the founder of Jazz Baul Records, an independent studio focused on music production, sound design, recording, mixing and mastering.

As an artist, he is now building his own body of music under the name AKRAM — songs shaped by memory, places, relationships and the quiet contradictions of everyday life.
